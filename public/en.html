How it works

  1. Hold your iPhone near the speakerSpotify, Deezer, Apple Music, vinyl or a friend's playlist: any source works.
  2. The track is recognisedIn a few seconds, with Apple's music recognition. Audio is never recorded.
  3. What's next appears, with the whyTracks that mix well, ranked: close BPM, compatible key, same style.

What it does

  • Auto-listen: leave the phone by the speaker and it recognises the track about every ten seconds.
  • A curated catalogue of about 5,000 tracks, each with BPM and key, from the 1960s to today.
  • Pop, dance and house, rock, French and US rap, French chanson, soul and funk, Latin, reggae.
  • Favourites boosted in the ranking, tracks you can hide, style and 'Popular' filters.
  • One-tap preview in Apple Music, Spotify or Deezer.
  • Unknown track? Add it: its BPM and key are looked up automatically.
  • Favourites synced between iPhone and iPad through iCloud.

FAQ

Do I need to be a DJ?

No. The app suggests, you choose. If you can make a playlist, you can use it.

Does it work with Spotify, Deezer, vinyl?

Yes: the iPhone listens to the room, whatever the source. Preview links open Apple Music, Spotify or Deezer.

Does it record conversations?

No. An acoustic fingerprint is computed on the iPhone to recognise the track, then the microphone stops. Nothing is kept.

Android or computer?

The web version works everywhere, with search by title (no listening). It installs like an app from Chrome on Android.

How much is it?

Nothing. No subscription, no ads, no account.

What if my track isn't in the catalogue?

Add it from the app: its BPM and key are looked up automatically, and everyone benefits.

Is the app in English?

The interface is French today; the English version is on its way.
